# Pickup review and checkout preparation

Inspect the cart's selected store and fulfillment method, each item's eligibility, available time slots, substitution controls, item prices, fees, taxes, and displayed estimated total. Do not change store, fulfillment, slot, or substitution settings without the user's direction.

Prepare a concise review containing the item list and quantities, unavailable items, proposed substitutions, selected store and pickup slot, fees/taxes, and latest estimated total. Point out price changes and any amount that is not yet known. Let the user make any final edits.

Stop at the review boundary. The user must explicitly approve the complete final order immediately before submission. Never press a control that submits the order, authorizes payment, or otherwise finalizes checkout. During evaluation, never place an order.

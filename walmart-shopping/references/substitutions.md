# Substitutions

When the requested product is explicitly unavailable for the selected store and fulfillment, look for candidates that preserve its material attributes. Compare brand, variant, package form, size, unit price, and pickup eligibility from the visible page.

State how each alternative differs from the request and why it may or may not fit. Keep materially different alternatives separate: fat percentage, dietary variant, meat cut, flavor, and fresh/frozen or liquid/powder form can change the result. Ask before proceeding when the difference affects a likely purchase preference. Never silently select or add a substitute.

If no suitable alternative is visible, report the gap and leave the item unresolved. Do not claim pickup unavailability based only on an empty or irrelevant search result.

# Rules

- Use hard edges and small, deliberate radii. Square corners are usually better.
- Favor visible outlines, inset shadows, and block-based layering over soft elevation.
- Use a limited palette with clear role colors and strong contrast.
- Keep iconography and decoration grid-aligned whenever possible.
- Let controls feel tactile: pressed, raised, selected, and disabled states should read clearly.
- Use bold section framing and chunky labels instead of airy minimalism.
- Avoid anti-aliased ornament when crisp blocks would do the job.

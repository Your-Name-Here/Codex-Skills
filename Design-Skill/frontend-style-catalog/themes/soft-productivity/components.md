# Components

- App shell: content-first, with quiet navigation and a strong reading column.
- Cards: medium padding, paper-like surfaces, modest elevation.
- Lists: emphasize grouping, progress, due dates, and notes without dense chrome.
- Forms: use readable labels, comfortable inputs, and clear focus states.
- Editors: give writing areas visual calm and stable margins.
- Empty states: friendly and small; prioritize next action over illustration.

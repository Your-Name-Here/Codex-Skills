# Rules

- Favor light or softly tinted surfaces with gentle contrast.
- Use generous but not wasteful spacing. Aim for calm, not emptiness.
- Keep typography highly readable with obvious rhythm between headings, body text, and metadata.
- Prefer paper, notebook, or card-stack cues over dashboard chrome.
- Use restrained accents for highlights, focus, and progress rather than for every surface.
- Let forms, lists, and writing areas breathe.
- Keep interaction feedback subtle but noticeable.

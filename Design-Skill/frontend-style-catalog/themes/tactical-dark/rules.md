# Rules

- Keep layout density medium-high. Prefer 8-12px gaps over oversized whitespace.
- Use dark neutral surfaces with one cool accent and one warning accent.
- Favor panels, split views, tables, inspectors, and stacked toolbars.
- Keep radii tight. Most elements should feel engineered, not soft.
- Make states obvious: hover, selected, active, disabled, loading, error.
- Use strong typography hierarchy without giant headings.
- Prefer borders and contrast shifts over blurred glass effects.
- Keep cards functional. Avoid oversized marketing-card proportions.
